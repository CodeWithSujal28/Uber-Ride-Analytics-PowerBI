# 🚗 Uber Ride Analytics | Power BI Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI data analytics project** built to analyze **150,000 Uber ride-booking records** and uncover insights into booking performance, revenue, customer behavior, vehicle performance, cancellations, payments, and locations.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to transform raw Uber booking data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that helps answer key business questions:

- How many bookings are successfully completed?
- How many bookings are lost and why?
- Which vehicle types perform best?
- Which payment methods generate the most Booking Value?
- Which locations have the highest ride activity?
- What are the major customer and driver cancellation reasons?
- How does customer and driver experience perform?

The analysis focuses on identifying **business problems, performance trends, and actionable insights** rather than simply visualizing the data.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A large number of ride-booking attempts do not result in completed rides due to cancellations, driver availability issues, and incomplete rides.

The business needs to understand:

- Booking conversion
- Revenue performance
- Vehicle contribution
- Customer behavior
- Cancellation patterns
- Driver availability
- High-demand locations

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of these areas to support better operational decision-making.

## 🔎 Key Business Insights

### 1. Booking Conversion

Only **62% of bookings were completed**, while approximately **38% were lost**.

This highlights booking conversion as one of the biggest operational opportunities.

### 2. Driver Cancellations

**Driver cancellations were the largest individual source of booking loss**, making driver availability and driver-booking matching important areas for improvement.

### 3. Vehicle Performance

**Auto** was the leading vehicle category by Booking Value and completed-booking contribution.

### 4. Payment Methods

**UPI** was the dominant payment method, contributing approximately **₹23.35M** in Booking Value.

### 5. Location Performance

- **Khandsa** was the top pickup location.
- **Ashram** was the top drop location.

These high-demand areas can be useful for driver positioning and supply optimization.

### 6. Customer Experience

The average ratings were:

- Customer Rating: **4.40 / 5**
- Driver Rating: **4.23 / 5**

Despite healthy ratings, the high booking-loss rate shows that operational efficiency remains an important improvement area.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Reduce driver cancellations through better driver-job matching.
- Improve driver availability in high-demand areas.
- Improve pickup-location accuracy to reduce customer cancellations.
- Use high-demand locations for strategic driver positioning.
- Focus on converting first-time riders into repeat customers.
- Monitor UPI payment performance closely.
- Track cancellation and incomplete-ride trends regularly.
